What you can do today

Practical steps from our recommendations. Nobody needs special equipment.

At homeSeparate wet, dry and recyclable waste. Keep oils and chemicals out of drains.
In shops and schoolsUse covered bins, cut extra packaging and hand waste only to authorised collectors.
For the PanchayatCollect waste regularly, keep drains clear and arrange water testing where needed.
Report itShare the exact location and a photo with the local office. Describe only what you can see.

Our photographs show visible waste only. Whether water is safe needs laboratory testing.
