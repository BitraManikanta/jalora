What Nellore can do

Do

Keep waste out of drains and water bodies. Segregate rubbish. Cut single-use plastic. Join organised cleanups. Report suspected pollution to the relevant local authority.

Stay safe

Never handle contaminated water or hazardous waste without protection. Report it instead.

Cleanup is not enough

Pollution returns unless its sources are controlled: wastewater treatment, proper disposal and regular monitoring.
